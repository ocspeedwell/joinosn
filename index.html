About Ochoudo Students Network

Ochoudo Students Network (OSN) is a non-partisan movement bringing together students from universities, polytechnics, colleges of education and other tertiary institutions across Imo State.

Empowering Students. Building Tomorrow.

OSN was established to inspire a new generation of young leaders through education, service, innovation and civic participation.

We believe students should not merely prepare for the future. They should help shape it by contributing ideas, leadership and community service today.

Guided by the vision of Ochoudo Martin Agbaso, OSN provides a platform where students can connect, learn, serve and become ambassadors of positive change throughout Imo State.
